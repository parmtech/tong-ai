# Governance charter — proposed, not yet ratified

## Pillars
Open specification and reference implementations; transparent decision records; elected or appointed maintainers accountable to contributors; independent safety/privacy oversight; public finances if a legal non-profit is formed; continued device-owner autonomy.

## Decision separation
- **Local node owners:** exclusive control over device allocation, installation, data permissions and exit.
- **Normal tasks:** locally replicated policy + regional schedulers; no global vote on every inference.
- **Protocol changes:** open RFC, tests, compatibility plan and review from security, privacy, infrastructure and model-evaluation maintainers.
- **Foundation finance:** legally accountable board, procurement/conflict-of-interest policy, signed expenditures, redacted public records; community input but no unauthorized access to accounts.
- **Emergency actions:** scoped network-release revocation, traffic isolation, documented incident resolution, expiration and independent retrospective. No permanent unaccountable override.

A decentralized intelligence design cannot guarantee perpetual existence, zero harm or unbounded improvement. The public charter seeks recoverability, survivability and meaningful human oversight.
