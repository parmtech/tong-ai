# TONG AI — 1,000 Deep Technical Questions and Answers

**Status:** architectural research FAQ; specifications are proposals, not proof of implementation. **Date:** 2026-11-02. **Language:** English to support a worldwide open-source community. **Format:** 25 domains × 10 distinct engineering mechanisms × 4 distinct review questions = exactly **1,000 question/answer pairs**.

The four angles for each mechanism are *technical design*, *threat/failure*, *measurement* and *production gate*. Each mechanism is individually curated for TONG AI; no answer is intended as a guarantee of hardware speed, privacy, universal intelligence or perpetual operation.
